Valuation input (v2 model) — aggregate P/E vs price-extension proxy

IC = correlation of the valuation z-score with the next 12-month log return of the risk asset, over the window where firm-level P/E exists (negative = high valuation preceded weaker returns). Overlapping windows: descriptive only, not a significance test.
